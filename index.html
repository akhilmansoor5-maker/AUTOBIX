Why AUTOBIX

Morethanacarwash.

Most places in the area will wash your car. Very few will decontaminate the paint first, correct it under proper light, and then protect it so the finish actually survives a Kerala monsoon. We built AUTOBIX to do the whole job in one place — and to do the unglamorous parts properly.

Separate tools for paint, glass, wheels and cabin. Dedicated bays so nothing is rushed. Everything checked under inspection lighting before we hand the keys back. And if something isn't right, it goes back in.

The lounge

While you wait

Good detailing takes hours, and that is usually the worst part of the experience. So we built the rest of the building around it — a coffee shop, a salon, an air-conditioned lounge and an accessories showroom, all under the same roof. Drop the car off, get something else done, and collect it finished.

  • Coffee Shop

    Proper coffee on site. Order, sit down and watch your car being worked on through the glass.

  • Beauty Salon

    A salon in the same building. Book an appointment for the same slot and get two things done in one trip.

  • Air-conditioned Lounge

    Sofas, AC and a television facing the bays — a comfortable place to wait rather than a plastic chair outside.

  • Accessories Showroom

    Browse alloys, mats, covers and audio while you wait, and have it fitted before you leave.

How it works

Fivesteps,nosurprises.

You always know what is being done, why, and what it costs before the first drop of water touches the car.

  1. 01

    Tell us the car

    Message us on WhatsApp with your model and what's bothering you. We'll tell you what it actually needs.

  2. 02

    Inspection on arrival

    We walk the car with you under light, point out the swirls, chips and wear, and agree the work before anything starts.

  3. 03

    Into the bay

    Your car goes into a dedicated bay with the right tools for the job. You wait in the lounge, or over a coffee.

  4. 04

    Checked under light

    Finished work is inspected under inspection lighting, not daylight. If it isn't right, it goes back.

  5. 05

    Handover & aftercare

    We show you what changed and how to keep it that way — what to use, what to avoid, when to come back.
